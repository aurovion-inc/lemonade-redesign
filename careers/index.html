Founding AI Engineer

  • Full-time
  • Hybrid, together every 2 to 3 months
  • $150k+
  • 0.75% to 2% equity
Apply by email

Own the agent harness and eval systems behind every game Lemonade makes, and lead a new project: an agent that works on one game for hours, not seconds.

What the work looks like

You'll own

  • The agent harness and eval infrastructure, end to end.
  • Testing new frontier and open-source models at launch, and tuning the harness to get the best out of each.
  • Feedback loops that turn real user failures into evals and deterministic checks.
  • A long-horizon project: an agent that builds a solid game scaffold over hours.

What matters

  • You treat the harness as a product, not a thin wrapper around an API.
  • You trust evals, traces and repeated experiments over impressive demos.
  • You move across the whole AI stack, and you can feel when a game loop is off.

After 3 months

Whole categories of agent mistakes are gone for hundreds of thousands of users, because you caught them and added checks.

Stack

Next.js, Convex, TypeScript. Claude and Cursor Agent SDKs, Fly, Docker.

Founding Design Engineer

  • Full-time
  • Hybrid, together every 2 to 3 months
  • $150k+
  • 0.5% to 1.25% equity
Apply by email

Own everything creators see and touch, and make something very complex feel as easy as play for hundreds of thousands of teenagers.

What the work looks like

You'll own

  • The path from idea to published game: onboarding, learning each creator's taste, getting them to a finished game faster.
  • The landing page, and the look and feel of Discord launches.
  • How parallel agents show their work, often a teenager's first close look at AI.

What matters

  • You notice the details most people skip, and they bother you until they're right.
  • You design for flow: the moment the tool disappears and making the game is just fun.
  • You'd rather explore what matters than execute someone else's spec.

After 3 months

Something you shipped got creators so excited that they brought their friends.

Teams we admire

Flora, Suno, VVD, Remy, Orchids and Manus.

Frontier Evals Engineer

  • Full-time
  • Hybrid, together every 2 to 3 months
  • $150k+
  • 0.5% to 1.25% equity
Apply by email

Measure what makes a generated game look and play well, and use it to steer the agent and post-training.

What the work looks like

You'll own

  • Hard, useful evals for visuals, gameplay and code.
  • Testing new models at launch, digging into failures, and recommending harness changes.
  • Rubrics and best practices, written with the Roblox expert scripters Lemonade already works with.
  • Sharing promising results with the 170K+ member Discord.

What matters

  • Evidence over vibes: evals, traces and repeated experiments.
  • Judgment about when to use deterministic checks and when to bring in experts.
  • Game taste, and real curiosity about what frontier models can do.

After 3 months

Your evals catch games that look or play badly, and when a new model ships you can say where it's better and what to change.

Stack

Next.js, Convex, TypeScript. Claude and Cursor Agent SDKs, Fly, Docker.
